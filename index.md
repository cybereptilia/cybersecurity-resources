Welcome! This blog collects explanations, reviews, and reference links for people working (or studying to work) in cybersecurity and IT operations. It was created for **CECS 6230 – IT Operations** at Polytechnic University of Puerto Rico.

**What you will find here:**

- Three in-depth explanations: **data recovery**, a **comparison of ITIL processes**, and **ethics, legislation, and outsourcing**.
- Two reviews of peer-reviewed papers, on **disaster recovery** and **incident response**.
- A [resources page]({{ "/resources/" | relative_url }}) with official guides, standards, and laws worth bookmarking.
