Official guides, standards, and laws that are useful for cybersecurity and IT operations professionals.

## Incident response

- [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3: Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management (2025)](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final): current U.S. guidance, organized around the six functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.
- [NIST Incident Response project](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/incident-response): supporting resources for preparation and the incident response life cycle.

## Backups, data recovery, and ransomware

- [CISA #StopRansomware Guide](https://www.cisa.gov/stopransomware/ransomware-guide): prevention best practices and a response checklist, including guidance on offline, encrypted, tested backups.
- [CISA: Back Up Business Data](https://www.cisa.gov/back-business-data): a short guide to the 3-2-1 backup rule for businesses.

## Laws and regulations

- [FTC Safeguards Rule amendment (Federal Register, 2023)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-11-13/html/2023-24412.htm): breach notification to the FTC for covered financial institutions.
- [Summary of U.S. state data breach notification statutes (Davis Wright Tremaine)](https://dwt.com/statedatabreachstatutes): all 50 states plus D.C., Guam, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands.
- [Puerto Rico Citizen Information on Data Banks Security Act (10 L.P.R.A. § 4051)](https://law.justia.com/codes/puerto-rico/2023/title-ten/subtitle-3/chapter-310/4051/): Puerto Rico's breach-notification law.

## Professional ethics

- [ISC2 Code of Ethics](https://www.isc2.org/ethics): the four canons every ISC2-certified professional agrees to follow.

## IT service management

- [ITIL 4 management practices: full list and purposes (ITSM.tools)](https://itsm.tools/34-itil-4-management-practices/): all 34 practices with short definitions.
